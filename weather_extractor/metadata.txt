name=Weather Extractor
qgisMinimumVersion=3.40
description=Extract NOAA/NWS AORC hourly gridded weather, observed USGS, Water Quality Portal, and NOAA GHCN daily and hourly data, and monthly OpenET/MODIS evapotranspiration for an area of interest in the contiguous United States, with CSV tables, GeoPackage station layers, and optional SWAT+ files.
version=2.1.0
author=Weather Extractor contributors
about=Windows bundle for QGIS 3.40 or later. Tab 1 (Area of Interest) defines the area of interest, elevation raster, output folder, target CRS, and timezone shared by all data sources. Tab 2 (Gridded Data) extracts NOAA/NWS AORC weather on the original ~800 m grid, on 5/10/50 km or custom mean grids, at the AOI centroid, or at the cells of the rain gauges found in tab 3, as daily and/or hourly CSV time series, including FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference ET, with optional SWAT+ climate files. Tab 3 (Observed Data) checks which USGS, Water Quality Portal, and NOAA GHCN data exist in the area (streamflow, gage height, groundwater, daily and hourly precipitation from USGS and NOAA rain gauges, channel geometry, sensor and sample water quality), previews the stations on the map, and downloads the selected datasets. Tab 4 (Remote Sensing Data) retrieves monthly actual evapotranspiration from OpenET and MODIS averaged over the area through Google Earth Engine (requires the Google Earth Engine plugin, signed in, and an Earth Engine Cloud project; Run lists any missing setup step). NOAA data attribution is requested; the plugin is not endorsed by NOAA.
