about=Windows bundle for QGIS 3.40 or later. For an area of interest, the plugin downloads NOAA AORC gridded weather (contiguous United States; hourly, ~800 m, from 1979; daily or hourly series with FAO-56 reference ET), USGS, EPA Water Quality Portal, and NOAA GHCN observed data (streamflow, water level, groundwater, rainfall, water quality), ANA HidroWeb daily discharge, water level, and rainfall (Brazil), and monthly OpenET and MODIS evapotranspiration through Google Earth Engine. Each data tab checks what exists in the area and period before downloading and previews the stations on the map. Outputs are CSV tables, GeoPackage station layers, and optional SWAT+ climate files. The plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, USGS, EPA, ANA, or Google.
