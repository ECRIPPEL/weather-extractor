about=For an area of interest, the plugin downloads NOAA AORC (contiguous United States; hourly, ~800 m, from 1979) or ECMWF ERA5-Land gridded weather (global; hourly, ~9 km, from 1950, also evaporation, runoff, and soil moisture) as daily or hourly series with FAO-56 reference ET, USGS, EPA Water Quality Portal, and NOAA GHCN observed data (streamflow, water level, groundwater, rainfall, water quality), ANA HidroWeb daily discharge, water level, and rainfall (Brazil), and monthly OpenET and MODIS evapotranspiration. Each data tab checks what exists in the area and period before downloading and previews the stations on the map. Outputs are CSV tables, GeoPackage station layers, and optional SWAT+ climate files. External dependencies: the AORC dataset needs the Python libraries xarray, zarr, numcodecs, fsspec, and aiohttp, which the plugin installs with pip into the QGIS profile on first use (internet access to pypi.org). ERA5-Land and the Remote Sensing tab need the Google Earth Engine plugin and a Google Cloud project registered for Earth Engine. The plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, ECMWF, USGS, EPA, ANA, or Google.
